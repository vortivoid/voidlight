# Commands:
## Basic:
- help: You literally just used it bruh you know what this does :sob:.
## Message Sending
- say [text]: Repeats whatever is sent following the command.
- uwuify [text]: Absolutely ruins the provided text and makes you want to gouge your eyes out.
## Voidglow:
- balance: Check your voidglow balance.
- transfer [@recipient] [amount]: Transfer a specified amount of voidglow from your balance to the recipient.
- daily: Claim your daily voidglow reward.
- shop: Lists all the items in the shop.
- buy [item name]: Buys an item from the shop.
## Fun:
- decide [choices]: Chooses a random word from a list seperated by spaces.
- rate [thing]: Rates the thing on a scale of 0-10.
- typecheck [pokemon]: Lists the type matchups against the specified pokemon.
- shittystory [names]: Writes random interactions between all the names entered.
- 8ball [question]: Responds to the question via a deep and meaningful thought process.
## Info:
- top: Lists server members by order of voidglow balance.
- streak: Gets your current daily streak.
- stats <@member>: Lists all tracked statistics on the user.
- badges <@member>: Lists all badges owned by the user.
## Games:
- coinflip <prediction> <bet>: Flip a coin with an optional prediction and bet amount.
- higherlower: Guess a number between 1-100. Win voidglow based on remaining attempts.
- hangman: Starts a game of Hangman where you guess letters to find the hidden word. Win voidglow based on the number of lives remaining.
- cancel: Cancels any ongoing games.
